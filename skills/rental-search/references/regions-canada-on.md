## Local facts

Checked 2026-11-06 on ontario.ca/laws (Residential Tenancies Act, 2006, "RTA"; O. Reg. 516/06; O. Reg. 9/18), ontario.ca guidance pages and toronto.ca. Re-check before quoting them as advice.

- **The only deposit allowed is a rent deposit** (often called last month's rent): at most one month's rent, requested on or before signing, applied to the last month, with interest paid every year at the guideline rate (RTA s. 105, 106). **Damage deposits and pet deposits are not allowed**; a lease term that requires one is void (s. 105, 134). A refundable key or fob deposit up to the replacement cost is allowed (O. Reg. 516/06 s. 17).
- **No fees** from a tenant or prospective tenant (premium, commission, "application fee" and similar) unless a regulation allows it (s. 134). A request for a damage deposit, pet deposit or application fee in Toronto is a warning sign.
- **Ontario Standard Lease** is required for most tenancies signed since 2018-04-30 (s. 12.1). If the landlord does not provide it within 21 days of a written request, the tenant may withhold up to one month's rent (s. 12.1) or end the lease early with notice (s. 47.0.1).
- **"No pets" clauses are void** (s. 14), except that condo rules banning certain pets can be enforced. A landlord can still act on a pet's behavior.
- **Rent increases**: the landlord and a new tenant agree the starting rent freely. After that, at most one increase every 12 months with 90 days' written notice (s. 116, 119), capped by the yearly guideline (2.1% for 2026, 1.9% for 2027) except in buildings first occupied after 2018-11-15, which have no cap (s. 6.1, 120).
- **Outside the Act**: homes where the tenant shares a kitchen or bathroom with the owner or the owner's close family living there (s. 5(i)), and vacation or seasonal stays (s. 5(a)). A furnished long-term rental is not exempt for being furnished; the Landlord and Tenant Board decides borderline mid-term stays on the facts.
- **Toronto short-term rentals**: stays under 28 days are short-term rentals, allowed only in the host's principal residence with a City registration number (format STR-0000-XXXXXX) on the listing; whole homes at most 180 nights a year (Toronto Municipal Code Ch. 547). For a furnished stay of under 28 days, a listing without a registration number is a warning sign.
- **Rooming houses**: since 2024-03-31, a house with 4 or more rooms rented separately with a shared kitchen or bathroom must be licensed (Ch. 575). Relevant when the shared-housing module is on.

## Toronto notes

- Many people commute by TTC (subway, streetcar, bus) or GO Transit. Ask the travel mode, and measure transit with `3e3` in the directions link (`references/scoring.md` → Commute) when that is how the person travels.
- "City of Toronto" on Kijiji covers the former boroughs (North York, Scarborough, Etobicoke, York, East York). Mississauga, Vaughan, Markham and others are separate cities; ask whether they count.
- Basement apartments are common and often listed as furnished. Check the ceiling height, windows and a separate entrance in the photos and description.
