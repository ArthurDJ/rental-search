## Local facts

Checked 2026-11-06 against the Residential Tenancy Act summaries on gov.bc.ca. Re-check before quoting them as advice.

- A security (damage) deposit may be at most half of one month's rent, and a pet damage deposit at most half of one month's rent (Residential Tenancy Act, s. 19).
- A landlord may not charge a fee for accepting a rental application (s. 15). An "application fee" request is a warning sign.
- The Act does not cover a home where the tenant shares a kitchen or bathroom with the owner (s. 4). Rooms in an owner's home have fewer protections; mention this when the shared-housing module is on.
- Condo (strata) buildings often charge a one-time move-in fee, typically around $200 in Kelowna listings seen on 2026-11-06; include it in the true monthly cost.

## Vancouver notes

Search pages opened on 2026-11-06; no listings were followed up and no commutes measured.

- Many people commute by transit (SkyTrain, buses). Ask the travel mode, and measure transit with `3e3` in the directions link (`references/scoring.md` → Commute) when that is how the person travels.
- "Vancouver" on most sites means the city only; Burnaby, Richmond, North Vancouver and others are separate cities with their own listings. Ask whether they count.
- Rooms in shared homes are a large share of furnished results; filter them out unless the shared-housing module is on.
